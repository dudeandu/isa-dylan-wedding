Parking Information

C Banquets · Mississauga Civic Centre (City Hall)
300 City Centre Drive, 12th Floor (Tower Elevators)
Mississauga, Ontario L5B 3C1

Complimentary parking is available after 6:00 PM and on weekends.

Weekday onsite parking is $1.00 per hour, to a maximum of $6.00 per day. Parking meter access begins at 7:00 AM.

If driving

  1. Enter the North Parking lot or Celebration Square lot from Duke of York Boulevard.
  2. Park on level P1 or P2.
  3. Enter the Civic Centre building through the blue doors marked “To Civic Centre Elevators.”
  4. Take the “Parking Garage Elevators” to the Ground Floor of the Civic Centre.
  5. Follow signs to the “Tower Elevators” on the Ground Floor.
  6. Take the “Tower Elevators” to the 12th Floor for C Banquets.

For GPS navigation: Enter “Mississauga Civic Centre Underground Parking” instead of C Banquets or 300 City Centre Drive to reach the underground parking lot.

If walking

  1. Enter the Civic Centre building (City Hall) through the West Entrance, facing Living Arts Drive.
  2. Take the “Tower Elevators” to the 12th Floor for C Banquets.
